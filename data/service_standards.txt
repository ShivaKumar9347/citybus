CITY BUS SERVICE STANDARDS DOCUMENT
=====================================
Version: 2024-01
Authority: City Transport Authority

SECTION 1: LOAD STANDARDS
--------------------------
1.1 Maximum Load Rule:
    - Peak hours (07:00-09:00, 16:00-19:00): Bus load must not exceed 80% capacity.
    - Off-peak hours: Bus load must not exceed 90% capacity.
    - Each bus has a standard capacity of 60 passengers.
    - If load exceeds the maximum, frequency must be increased.

1.2 Overcrowding Definition:
    - A time band is considered overcrowded if the passenger load per bus exceeds the maximum allowed load.
    - Overcrowded bands must be revised within the next planning cycle.

SECTION 2: WAITING TIME STANDARDS
-----------------------------------
2.1 Maximum Waiting Time Rules:
    - Peak hours: Maximum waiting time between buses = 10 minutes (frequency >= 6 buses/hour).
    - Off-peak hours: Maximum waiting time = 15 minutes (frequency >= 4 buses/hour).
    - Late evening (20:00-21:00): Maximum waiting time = 20 minutes (frequency >= 3 buses/hour).

2.2 Minimum Frequency:
    - No time band shall have fewer than 2 buses per hour.
    - Fleet limit must not be exceeded at any time.

SECTION 3: FLEET MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------
3.1 Fleet Limit Rule:
    - The number of buses deployed per hour must not exceed the route's fleet limit.
    - Fleet limit is set by the operations department and cannot be changed without approval.

3.2 Frequency Calculation:
    - Required buses = ceil(ridership / (capacity * max_load_factor))
    - If required buses > fleet_limit, cap at fleet_limit and log overcrowding.

SECTION 4: REVISION RULES
---------------------------
4.1 Timetable Revision Triggers:
    - Simulated load exceeds maximum load standard.
    - Waiting time exceeds the standard for that period.
    - Passenger complaints about specific time bands.

4.2 Revision Protocol:
    - Operations Manager reviews simulation results.
    - Each revision must cite the specific standard clause violated.
    - Revised timetable takes effect the following day.

SECTION 5: FESTIVAL/SPECIAL EVENT STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------
5.1 Festival Day Rule:
    - On festival days, expected ridership may spike by 40-80%.
    - Fleet deployment can be increased by borrowing from low-demand routes.
    - Maximum waiting time standards still apply.
    - Operations Manager must approve any inter-route fleet transfer.

5.2 Demand Spike Handling:
    - If ridership exceeds 150% of normal, emergency buses may be requested.
    - Festival timetable must be published 48 hours in advance.
